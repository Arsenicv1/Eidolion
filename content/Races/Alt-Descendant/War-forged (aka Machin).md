
Machines made by past humankind to imitate man and eventually became a sentient species. the art of making new Machin has been lost so the best they can do is use old parts, try to repair old ones, or make Prototypes. 

### Arc-Machin -Rare
Original Machin from before the fall of Old Aurelia typically with damaged or even wiped memories, quite rare and even so there are no perfect condition Arcs remaining.

### Reforge-Machin -Common
Machin made from working parts of broken Arc and jerry rigged work arounds. very common in Aurelia.

### Proto-Machin -Uncommon
Proto are extremely new and have only been around for 25 years. because of being manufacturable in recent years they are only able to do simple tasks. they are quite large and clunky lacking the eloquence of the arch.
