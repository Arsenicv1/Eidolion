
vampires are less of a race and more of a trait, vampire Masters are those who either ate a large chunk of one of the original dragons or those who ate someone who did, granting them immense power at a cost. 

there are 5 Vampire Masters the strongest vampire lords, each with different aspects and abilities


1. [[Aris Volan Borland]], Arcane Chosen
2. Victor Ranin Nepith, Mind Malice
3. [[Morra Eilean l'acier]], The Machinist
4. Nicholas Ulkia Nepith, The Flesh Maker
5. [[Xandros Theocles Carmine]], The Savior
