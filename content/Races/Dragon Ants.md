These people hail from the deserts of the [[Holy Empire of Invictora]]. While the country is vast, it is filled with three types of dragon ant: Abyssal, Crimson and Pale. All of which were created by [[Solin, Lord of Flame and Justice]] by combining ants with the blood of one of the [[Original Dragons]].
The specific process of this involves that the ants of the desert ate the dragon's corpse, once it was devoured the ants started evolving for 200 years, after which they acquired their appearance and abilities.
