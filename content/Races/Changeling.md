The Changeling is completely unique from the other races as a changeling has never been born but instead they are created by being granted an alternative for a soul a "Wild Spark". it is difficult to contain making low success rates on creation. fickle is that spark, making the original form lost and cursed to use the face of another. 

The original form aka the vessel must be able to house a soul but not molded by one (a once living/sentient creature that had not lived for greater than 500 moons), because of those requirements it is rare that the ritual is preformed at all. 

As they are soulless traditional spell casting is impossible, the only way is through intense training and even then they will never fully master that which is wild.
