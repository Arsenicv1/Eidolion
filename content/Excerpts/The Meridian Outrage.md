"Near the new age, while other countries were struggling with great threats the Royals of Meridith refused to help the countries asking for aid, causing a rift in the civilians who formed two groups, leading to a civil war. the Bones believed in helping their fellows and the Doubloons believed that helping others would be throwing away their chance of glory. the two groups waged war for over 115 years, having killed the royals they formed an republic and found a way to agreement. now the Bones and Doubloons still occasionally clash but will find a middle ground" - Excerpt from Into to Meridian Politics
