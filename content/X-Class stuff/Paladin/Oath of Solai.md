_Empowers the will of Justice and Flame_

The Oath of Solia binds Paladins to the ideal of justice. These Paladins meet the archetype of the knight in shining armor. They hold themselves to the highest standards of conduct, and some—for better or worse—hold the rest of the world to the same standards.

Those who swear this oath are devoted to Solin and use their god's tenets as the measure of personal devotion.

These paladins share the following tenets:

- Must uphold Law and order.
- Let no bad deeds be unpunished.
- Fight for the righteous, defend those who cannot help themselves.

#### Level 3: Hour of Judgement 

As a [Magic](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#MagicAction) action, you can expend one use of your Channel Divinity to call for Solin's Judgement (he may deny) and target a creature, if Solin deems that creature a sinner, until that creature dies or 1 minute passes you gain one following buffs (Chosen by DM): 

- Solin grants you +0.5x fire affinity against the sinner, if you do not have a fire cantrip you gain one.
- Solin blesses you in combat, any melee attack deals an additional 1d8 fire damage against the sinner.
- Solin protects you with 1d6 temporary hp per turn.
- Solin bestows you with all three against the sinner.

#### Level 3: Oath of Solai Spells

The magic of your oath ensures you always have certain spells ready; when you reach a Paladin level specified in the Oath of Solai Spells table, you thereafter always have the listed spells prepared.

#### Level 7: Aura of Radiance 

When a non-ally creature starts their turn within or when entering the Aura of Protection they take 1d4 fire damage, once per turn.

#### Level 15: Solar Blade

As a bonus action for 1 minute you may manifest Solai's power in a weapon that is equipped transforming it into Manifestation of Aurora. Once you use this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a [Long Rest](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#LongRest).
