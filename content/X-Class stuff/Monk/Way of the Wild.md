Play with Chance and Chaos

Way of the Wild Monk Specializes in confusing both side of the battle by causing wilder wild magic to go off from their attacks and ability's.

Features: Wild Hands, Unpredictable fist, Wild strikes, Mana Influence, Luck for later

#### Level 3: Wild Hands

When ever you hit a target with a melee attack or cast a spell you roll a d10 if you roll a **6** it becomes "**wild**" and you must roll a d20 to decide what table you roll from. if you roll a 1 roll on the **Disaster** table, if you roll a 2-7 you roll on the **Minor Inconvenience** table, if you roll a 8-13 you roll on the **True Neutral** table, if you roll a 14-19 you roll on the **Minor Convenience** table, and if you roll a 20 you roll on the **Blessing** table.

#### Level 3: Unpredicible Fist

whenever you make an attack that misses you may use your reaction and a focus point to roll your Martial Arts die and add it to your attack to possibly change the outcome this turn.

#### Level 3: Spellcasting

You have learned to cast spells.

_**Cantrips.**_ You know two cantrips of your choice from the sorcerer spell list. Whenever you gain a Monk level, you can replace one of these cantrips with another cantrip of your choice from the Sorcerer spell list.

When you reach Monk level 10, you learn another Sorcerer cantrip of your choice.

_**Spell Slots.**_ The Way of the Wild Fist Spellcasting table shows how many spell slots you have to cast your level 1+ spells. You regain all expended slots when you finish a [Long Rest](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#LongRest).

**_Prepared Spells of Level 1+._** You prepare the list of level 1+ spells that are available for you to cast with this feature. To start, choose three level 1 spells from the Sorcerer spell list.

The number of spells on your list increases as you gain Monk levels, as shown in the Prepared Spells column of the Way of the Wild Fist Spellcasting table. Whenever that number increases, choose additional spells from the Sorcerer spell list until the number of spells on your list matches the number on the table. The chosen spells must be of a level for which you have spell slots. For example, if you’re a level 7 Monk, your list of prepared spells can include five Sorcerer spells of levels 1 and 2 in any combination.

**_Changing Your Prepared Spells._** Whenever you gain a Monk level, you can replace one spell on your list with another Sorcerer spell for which you have spell slots.

**_Spellcasting Ability._** Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for your Sorcerer spells.

**_Spellcasting Focus._** You can use your hands as a Spellcasting Focus for your Sorcerer spells.

#### Level 6: Wild Strikes

Whenever you would use your Flurry of Blows, you may instead use a Wild Strikes attack

**_Wild Strikes._** You can expend 1 Focus Point to make three Unarmed Strikes as a Bonus Action, when you hit a creature you may force a **Wild Effect** to trigger roll a d20 to determine which table you roll on as stated in the Wild Fist Feature.

#### Level 11: Mana Influence

When you hit a caster(or Non-caster), you may spend 2 focus points to give them **Unregulated Mana(Self Regulating Mana), they must make a CON save (equal to your spell save DC) each turn, if they pass the effect ends early.**

**(Caster) Unregulated Mana:** while you have this effect every time you cast a spell or cantrip you must roll a d6 on a 6 it becomes WILD and must roll a d20 to determine what table you roll on.

**(Non-caster) Self Regulating Mana:** while you have this effect every time you get hit by an attack you must roll a d6 on a 6 it Activates your WILD MANA and must roll a d20 to determine what table you roll on.

#### Level 17: Luck for Later

Whenever after you roll a d12 to determine which wild magic table you roll on, you may store the result for 4 Focus points, for up to 5 turns depending on the type stored you gain the following buffs, if you expend the result setting you back to none you use a better ability:

##### Blessings

Blessings: while you have this type stored you may: once per turn you may Cast Cure wounds  at 1st level for a free action.

Expend: you may Cast cure wounds at 5th level for a free action.

##### Disaster

Disaster: while you have this type stored you may: deal an additional 1d6 necrotic damage each attack.

Expend: deal 3d8 additional on your next attack.

##### Minor Conveniences

Minor Conveniences: while you have this type stored you have this buff: when you miss an attack still do 1 bludgeoning damage to the target.

Expend: regain a focus point per attacks you've hit this turn.

##### Minor Inconveniences

Minor Inconveniences: while you have this type stored you may: if an attack missed you, you may make it hit and gain advantage on your next attack toward that creature.

Expend: give a chosen creature disadvantage till end of their turn.

##### None

You have no Wild roll stored right now.

##### True Neutral-DEX

True Neutral: while you have this type stored you Have this buff: +2 to either your DEX or STR you choose

Expend: cause a target to roll on a wild table of your choice.

##### True Neutral-STR

True Neutral: while you have this type stored you Have this buff: +2 to either your DEX or STR you choose

Expend: cause a target to roll on a wild table of your choice.
