The second kin of [[Ferra, Lord of Death and Destruction]], absolutely detests his mother and sister [[Niva, Lord of Greed and Consumption]], instead follows in his uncle [[Solin, Lord of flame and justice]]'s footsteps and fights with honor above all else, as such he detests those without honor. his form resembles a Drow with a crescent engraved in his skull wearing typical light armors.
