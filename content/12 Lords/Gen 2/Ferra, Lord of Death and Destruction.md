The final kin of [[E'taria, Lord of essence]], she looks exactly like her mother but unlike her she holds no love in her soul not even for her own kin, she will act as if she cares if it will help her get her goal. she is known by mortals as the one who collects what is due, and if you are unfortunate enough to owe her your soul will be consumed by hate. her kin are: [[Niva, Lord of Greed and Consumption]], [[Luthil, Lord of Combat and Shettar]]
